Antarctica

Antarctic test cases in the present-day climate (RACMO2.3p2 1981–2010, ISMIP7 ocean) with the snapshot backend: a 15-kyr spin-up at 32 km that optimizes the basal friction (cb_ref) and the ocean thermal-forcing correction (tf_corr), with the marine shelf on a 16 km grid (yelmox_Antarctica.nml, the base of a multigrid resolution test), and a 50-kyr present-day run at 32 km without optimization (the same par file with -p overrides). Two variants run on the ESM backend (yelmox_esm_Antarctica.nml, alone and with the nudge setup of scripts/nudge/run_nudge_antarctica.sh).

  • Program: yelmox (make yelmox) with the snapshot backend ([comps] climate = "snapshots", see Snapshots); the ESM variants with [comps] climate = "esm" (see ESM forcing).
  • Config: yelmox/yelmox_Antarctica.nml (the present-day run: -p, see Running), yelmox/yelmox_esm_Antarctica.nml (the nudge variant: -p).
  • Scripts: yelmox/run_mg_resolution.sh, yelmox/run_mg_cfmin.sh, yelmox/run_mg_forward.sh, all on yelmox_Antarctica.nml; scripts/nudge/run_nudge_antarctica.sh (nudge_ant) on yelmox_esm_Antarctica.nml.

Running

From the yelmox root:

runme -rs -q compute -w 02:00:00 --omp 16 -e yelmox -n yelmox/yelmox_Antarctica.nml -o output/ant
runme -rs -q compute -w 08:00:00 --omp 16 -e yelmox -n yelmox/yelmox_Antarctica.nml -o output/ant-pd \
      -p ctrl.time_end=50e3 sim.relax=False sim.opt=False domain.grid_hub=ANT-32KM domain.grid_ice="" \
         domain.grid_shelf=ANT-32KM marine_shelf.tf_correction=True tm_2D.dt=500 tm_2Dsm.dt=500

On a busy queue, run them as chains of short jobs (Long runs in chunks). The multigrid resolution test, from anywhere; run_mg_forward.sh needs the restart bundles of run_mg_resolution.sh:

yelmox/run_mg_resolution.sh
yelmox/run_mg_cfmin.sh
yelmox/run_mg_forward.sh

The scripts submit by default (runopts=-r runs locally, runopts=-s stages only). The climate always runs on ANT-32KM; Yelmo, isostasy and the surface on the Yelmo grid; the hub and the marine shelf on the shelf grid.

  • run_mg_resolution.sh: six 25-kyr spin-ups (ctrl.time_end=25e3) into output/mg_opt/y<ice>_m<shelf>: Yelmo and shelf on the same grid (ANT-8KM, ANT-16KM, ANT-32KM), and the shelf on a finer grid (Yelmo 32 km with shelf 8 or 16 km, Yelmo 16 km with shelf 8 km). 2D output every 1 kyr, ytopo.gz_Hg1=100; shared queue, 10 h.
  • run_mg_cfmin.sh: the three Yelmo-32KM spin-ups again with the friction floor ytill.cf_min=1e-4 (par file: 0.01), into output/mg_opt_cfmin1e-4; compute queue, 8 h.
  • run_mg_forward.sh: 1-kyr runs from restart-25.000-kyr of the three Yelmo-32KM spin-ups in output/mg_opt (a missing bundle is skipped), with sim.relax=False, sim.opt=False, yelmo.restart_relax=0: ctrl (no anomaly) and warm (+2 K air and ocean temperature: the uniform term [term_dT] on the constant series [series_dT], f_ta = 1, f_to = 1), into output/mg_fwd/<case>_<ctrl|warm>; compute queue, 4 h.

Spin-up

  • Timeline: [ctrl] 0 to 15 kyr, dtt = 10 yr, the forcing held at present day (tstep_method = "const", tstep_const = 0), present-day insolation ([orbit] method = "time", time = 0); the sea level follows the model’s own ice-volume change ([barysealevel] method = "fastiso").
  • Cold start: a 10-yr equilibration ([sim] init_method = "equil", init_equil_time = 10).
  • Optimization ([sim] opt = True): cb_ref (opt_cf = "L21", from cf_init = 1e-2 everywhere) and tf_corr per point (opt_tf = "L21-points", tf_sigma = 50 km, within ±1 K), both until 15 kyr ([opt] cf_time_end, tf_time_end); the grounding line and grounding zone relax towards the observations until 6 kyr ([sim] relax = True, [relax] topo_rel = 4, tau1 = 100 yr until 2 kyr, then towards tau2 = 2000 yr).
  • Basin corrections: [marine_shelf] corr_method = "tf", corr_names = "b1" "b12" "b14" "b15", corr_values = 0.3 0.3 -1.5 0.0 on the Zwally2012 basins 1 (Ronne), 12 (Ross), 14 (Pine Island) and 15 (Abbott); corr_b<k> selects basin k.
  • Output: 1D every year, 2D every 200 yr, small 2D every 50 yr.

Climate

Present day only ([forcing] series = "", terms = ""). run_mg_forward.sh adds the constant series [series_dT] (method = "const", 0) and the uniform term [term_dT] of input/forcing_db.nml (forcing.series=dT forcing.terms=dT): it sets series_dT.f_min and f_max and the term’s fields with -p, so runme copies the groups into the staged par file.

Present day

0 to 50 kyr, no relaxation, no optimization ([sim] relax = False, opt = False), every component on ANT-32KM. tf_corr is read from a file ([marine_shelf] tf_correction = True: dT_nl of {grid_name}_OCEAN_ISMIP6_J20.nc). 2D and small 2D output every 500 yr.

ESM variants

Two phases in calendar years ([ctrl] calendar = True, calendar_ref = 2000): [spinup] (the par file’s run_step), 0 to 20 kyr with the forcing held at 2020 (tstep_const) and the reference climatology only, cb_ref and tf_corr optimized until 20 kyr; and [transient], on calendar time with dtt = 1 yr. Both read the reference RACMO2.3p2 (ERA5-forced, 1990–2019, detrended) and ORAS5 1990–2019 (detrended), with [esm] experiment = "ctrl".

yelmox_esm_Antarctica.nml nudge (nudge_ant)
Grids ANT-8KM for every component ANT-16KM for every component
[esm] par_file input/esm/esm_ant.nml (ISMIP6-2300) input/esm/esm_nudge.nml (Nudge_Skiba26, years 4501–5000)
[esm] use_esm False True
[transient] 1020 to 3500 4501 to 5000
Surface mass balance chion ITM (nml_itm = "itm") chion PDD ([pdd_chion], cg05)
[esm] experiment "ctrl" "ctrl" (reference only) or "nudgedhigh03" with use_proj = True: the anomaly of the nudged AWI-ESM run minus its PI run, 3501–5000 (atmosphere; the ocean has no nudged files on ANT-16KM yet)

The nudge variant runs in two steps, from the yelmox root: scripts/nudge/run_nudge_antarctica.sh spinup, then scenarios (ctrl and nudgedhigh03, from the spin-up’s last restart bundle; STAGE=1 stages only).

The reference climate is the mean over time_ref (1990–2019) of the RACMO2.3p2 1979–2022 and ORAS5 1979–2024 monthly series. On ANT-8KM the ORAS5 reference needs about 15 GB: run with --omp 32 or more (the memory follows the cores).

Configuration

Grids hub and marine shelf ANT-16KM; Yelmo, isostasy, climate and surface ANT-32KM (pd: ANT-32KM for every component)
Topography BedMachine v4 ({grid_name}_TOPO-BedMachine-v4.nc)
Reference climate (snapshot_pd) RACMO2.3p2 1981–2010 (temperature, precipitation, elevation); ocean ISMIP7 climatology (FesmData Ocean-ISMIP7)
Surface mass balance chion PDD ([chion] model = "pdd", [pdd_chion] pdd_method = "cg05", temperature_sigma = 5 K)
Shelf melt bmb_method = "quad-nl", tf_method = 1; grounding-line melt [ytopo] bmb_gl_method = "pmpt"
Calving calv_flt_method = calv_grnd_method = "equil" (level set, calving rate opposite to the ice velocity)
Dynamics DIVA, regularized Coulomb friction (beta_method = 3), beta_gl_scale = 2 (Zstar)
Isostasy FastIsostasy LV-ELVA (method = 3), Earth structure of the isostasy grid ([isos] rheology_file = ".../{grid_name}_GIA_HR24.nc": ANT-32KM_GIA_HR24.nc)
Geothermal heat flux {grid_name}_GHF-Shapiro2004.nc
Present-day data RACMO2.3/ERA-Interim hybrid 1981–2010 smb and surface temperature, velocity Rignot2011 ([yelmo_data])
Named regions yelmo_ts_APIS/WAIS/EAIS.nc
