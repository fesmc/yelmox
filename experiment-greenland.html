Greenland

Greenland test cases at 16 km, without optimization of the basal friction or the ocean thermal forcing: the present-day reference climate with the PMIP3 piControl and LGM snapshots weighted by the series alpha, an orbital record (yelmox_Greenland.nml), the same with a homogeneous air temperature ramp as a functionality test at 32 km, and the present-day reference alone (both yelmox_Greenland.nml with -p overrides).

  • Program: yelmox (make yelmox) with the snapshot backend ([comps] climate = "snapshots", see Snapshots).
  • Config: yelmox/yelmox_Greenland.nml; the ramp and present-day runs set their differences with -p (see Running).

Running

Run from the yelmox root:

runme -rs -q compute -w 02:00:00 --omp 16 -e yelmox -n yelmox/yelmox_Greenland.nml -o output/grl
runme -rs -q compute -w 02:00:00 --omp 16 -e yelmox -n yelmox/yelmox_Greenland.nml -o output/grl-ramp \
      -p ctrl.tstep_method=rel domain.grid_hub=GRL-32KM domain.grid_isos=GRL-32KM domain.grid_clim=GRL-32KM \
         domain.grid_surface=GRL-32KM domain.grid_shelf=GRL-32KM regions.basin_sets=Zwally2012 tm_2D.dt=50 \
         forcing.series==alpha,dTo,ramp forcing.terms==lgm,ocean,ramp
runme -rs -q compute -w 08:00:00 --omp 16 -e yelmox -n yelmox/yelmox_Greenland.nml -o output/grl-pd \
      -p ctrl.time_end=50e3 regions.basin_sets=Zwally2012 tm_2D.dt=500 tm_2Dsm.dt=500 \
         forcing.series="" forcing.terms=""

On a busy queue, run them as chains of short jobs (Long runs in chunks). The forcing time of yelmox_Greenland.nml is set with -p: e.g. ctrl.tstep_const=-21e3 holds the climate of 21 ka, ctrl.tstep_method=rel ctrl.time_init=-130e3 ctrl.time_end=0 follows the index through the last glacial cycle.

Timeline

  • yelmox_Greenland.nml: [ctrl] 0 to 15 kyr, dtt = 10 yr, the forcing held at present day (tstep_method = "const", tstep_const = 0), where at = 0: the climate is the present-day reference.
  • Ramp: the same on relative time (tstep_method = "rel"), so that the ramp advances with the model time.
  • Present day: 0 to 50 kyr.
  • All: cold start on the present-day topography ([sim] init_method = "none"), no relaxation, no optimization ([sim] relax = False, opt = False; [relax] and [opt] are unused). Present-day insolation ([orbit] method = "time", time = 0); the sea level follows the model’s own ice-volume change ([barysealevel] method = "fastiso").
  • Output: 1D every year; 2D every 200 yr (ramp: 50 yr; pd: 500 yr), small 2D every 50 yr (pd: 500 yr).

Climate: snapshots weighted by alpha

The reference pd plus the term lgm ([term_lgm], picontrol -> lgm on tas and pr): the temperature adds the PMIP3 LGM – piControl anomaly scaled by the series alpha, the precipitation is multiplied by their ratio. The PMIP3 snapshots give the annual mean and JJA temperature (monthly = False) and the annual precipitation, smoothed over 50 km at loading (clim_sigma = 50). The precipitation-temperature scaling is [snapshots] f_p = 0.06 (default 0.05).

The series [series_alpha] of the par file is the orbital record input/alpha_orbital_r2020.dat (method = "series", 300 ka to present), normalised to 0 at present (norm_t0 = 0) and 1 at 21 ka (norm_t1 = -21e3). The ocean (ORAS5) has no snapshot term: the term ocean adds the derived series dTo, 0.25 times the mean sea-level air temperature anomaly over the domain ([series_dTo], from = "air_anomaly", region = "all").

Air temperature ramp

The ramp run adds the series ramp and the uniform term ramp of input/forcing_db.nml (forcing.series==alpha,dTo,ramp, forcing.terms==lgm,ocean,ramp; [term_ramp] on tas and pr with f_ta = 1):

  • Series: [series_ramp] method = "ramp-slope", df_dt_max = 0.0025 yr⁻¹ from f_min = 0 to f_max = 5: the air temperature rises by 5 K over 2000 yr and stays there; white noise of sigma = 0.3 on the series; with_kill = False.
  • Response: the precipitation is multiplied by exp(f_p·dTa); the ocean follows through the term ocean, whose sea-level anomaly includes dTa.

Present day

The present-day run keeps the reference only (forcing.series="", forcing.terms=""). Its basin sets are Zwally2012 only (no NEGIS).

Configuration

Grids GRL-16KM for every component (ramp: GRL-32KM)
Topography BedMachine v6 ({grid_name}_TOPO-BedMachine-v6.nc)
Ice domain [yelmo_masks] mask_ice_dynamic = "region:Greenland,Canadian_Arctic_Archipelago"
Reference climate (snapshot_pd) MAR v3.14 1961–1990 (temperature, snowfall + rainfall, elevation); ocean ORAS5 1981–2010
Snapshots (snapshot_picontrol, snapshot_lgm) PMIP3 piControl and LGM ensemble means, global (GLOBAL-1DEG, remapped, 50 km smoothing; atmosphere only, the ocean follows the term ocean)
Surface mass balance chion ITM ([comps] surface_method = "chion", [chion] nml_itm = "itm_chion", alb_ice = 0.31)
Shelf melt bmb_method = "lin", tf_method = 1
Calving calv_flt_method = calv_grnd_method = "equil" (level set, calving rate opposite to the ice velocity)
Dynamics DIVA, regularized Coulomb friction (beta_method = 3, beta_q = 0.2, beta_u0 = 100 m/yr)
Isostasy FastIsostasy LV-ELRA (method = 2), uniform mantle (10²¹ Pa s, tau = 3000 yr)
Geothermal heat flux constant 50 mW m⁻² ([ghf] use_obs = False)
Present-day data MAR v3.14 1961–1990 smb and surface temperature, velocity Joughin2018, isochrones MacGregor2025 ([yelmo_data])
