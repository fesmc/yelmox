yelmox

The YelmoX program: one ice-sheet domain with FastIsostasy bedrock, a shared barystatic sea level, a climate backend chosen at runtime, a surface mass balance and the marine shelf, built on the multigrid kryos_domain (see Multigrid coupling). Its variant yelmox_bipolar runs two domains, north and south, coupled through the sea level and an ocean box model.

Program Build Domains Climate Ocean
yelmox make yelmox (rembo=1 for REMBO) one any backend ([comps] climate) the climate (depth profiles or shelf base)
yelmox_bipolar make yelmox_bipolar north + south snapshots (×2) snapshots + shared ocean box model
  • Program: yelmox/yelmox.f90 (thin driver) + libs/kryos*.f90 (domain, coupling, startup, output).
  • Build: make yelmox (make yelmox rembo=1 to link REMBO).
  • Configs: yelmox/yelmox_<domain>.nml (Antarctica, Greenland, North, LIS, Pyrenees, SRG, plus pd_ present-day and paleo variants), yelmox_esm_*.nml (ESM forcing), yelmox_rembo_Greenland.nml (REMBO), yelmox_Greenland_ramp.nml (an air temperature ramp: [anom] on a ramp-slope index).
  • Experiments: Greenland, Antarctica, ISMIP7 projections, 1pctCO2, TIPMIP, Antarctic paleo, Greenland paleo, Laurentide, Mountain glaciers.

Components

Role Module Grid
Ice sheet Yelmo grid_ice
Isostasy + sea level FastIsostasy (isos) + shared bsl grid_isos
Climate (atmosphere + ocean) backend of [comps] climate: snapshots, esm or rembo grid_clim
Surface mass balance chion (default), smbpal, smb_simple or the climate’s own grid_surface
Sub-shelf melt marine_shelf grid_shelf
Geometry hub htopo grid_hub (hi-res)

Each module runs on its own grid, set in [domain]; the coupler remaps fields between grids at the moment of coupling. See Multigrid coupling.

Climate backends

The forcing of a run is set at runtime by its climate backend, [comps] climate:

Backend Supplies Page
snapshots atmosphere + ocean profiles, from climate snapshots blended by indices, one blend model over any number of snapshots Snapshots
esm reference climatology + Earth-system-model anomalies; atmosphere or smb, ocean at the shelf base, subglacial discharge ESM forcing
rembo REMBOv1 atmosphere + smb; ocean from snapshots (ocean-only snapshot) REMBO climate

Stepping order

The driver owns the timeline (ts) and the shared sea level (bsl), and advances the domain once per step with the coupling sequence written out in the time loop:

call step_relax(dom, ts)          ! topography relaxation (relax)
call step_optimize(dom, ts)       ! cb_ref/tf_corr optimization (opt)
call step_isostasy(dom, ts, bsl)  ! bedrock + sea level (dt_isos cadence)
call couple_to_yelmo(dom)         ! bedrock now; smb + shelf melt lag one step
call step_ice_bnd(dom, ts, idx)   ! prescribed ice in the fixed/relaxation cells ([ice_bnd])
call step_icesheet(dom, ts)       ! yelmo_update
call couple_yelmo_to_htopo(dom)   ! hi-res geometry from the models
call step_climate(dom, ts, idx)   ! climate (dt_clim cadence)
call step_surface(dom, ts)        ! surface mass balance + temperature
call step_shelf(dom, ts)          ! shelf-base melt + temperature

couple_to_yelmo assembles the Yelmo boundary state: isostasy from this step, smb and shelf melt from the previous step (a one-step coupling lag); step_climate, step_surface and step_shelf then produce the forcing consumed on the next step. The climate is refreshed on the comps.dt_clim cadence, isostasy on the comps.dt_isos cadence (the bedrock is held in between); the smb every step.

Indices and the homogeneous anomaly ([indices], [anom])

Indices are named scalar series, each a tsgen time-series generator (the modern replacement for the legacy hyster module). They are driver-owned: the program holds one registry (indices_class, idx, libs/kryos_forcing.f90), advances every index once per step on the forcing clock time_rel, and passes it to step_climate (and the cold start); consumers read an index by name (the snapshots axes, [anom]). Multi-domain drivers share one registry.

&indices
    names = "ramp"            ! "" = no indices
/

&idx_ramp                     ! a tsgen group (unset keys: template [idx])
    method    = "ramp-time"   ! const | ramp-slope | ramp-time | ramp-time-step | sin | series | exp | PI42 | ...
    f_min     = 0.0
    f_max     = 5.0
    dt_ramp   = 200.0
    ! ... (see the [idx] template of input/yelmox_defaults.nml)
    ! units = "K" (label of idx_<name> in the 1D output, default "1"),
    ! restart_every_df = 0.5 (a restart bundle each |Δf|, default 0 = off)
/

&anom
    active = True     ! a homogeneous anomaly from one index
    index  = "ramp"
    f_ta   = 1.0      ! dTa = f * f_ta   (air temperature [K])
    f_to   = 0.0      ! dTo = f * f_to   (ocean temperature [K])
    f_so   = 0.0      ! dSo = f * f_so   (ocean salinity [psu])
/

Time-driven methods (ramp-*, sin, const, series) are analytic or tabulated; feedback methods (exp, PI/PID controllers) modulate the forcing rate from the model response — the response variable passed to tsgen is total ice volume (Gt). The run stops when any index trips its kill switch (with_kill), and writes a restart bundle restart-<n> each time an index moves by more than its restart_every_df. Each index restarts from idx_<name>_restart.nc in the bundle, and is written to the 1D output as idx_<name> (plus idx_<name>_df_dt).

[anom] is a climate modifier (libs/climate_mods.f90): applied to the backend’s output after the backend, before [ocn_fraction], [ocn_obm], [taper] and [bias], on the climate grid. Air temperature + dTa, precipitation × exp(β·dTa) (β the backend’s precipitation sensitivity), ocean profiles + dTo/dSo (esm: at the shelf base). The group is optional (inactive without it) and takes the domain suffix (anom<sfx>). Checked at init: the index must be in the registry, and a backend that supplies the smb (esm with surface_method = "climate") refuses it. rembo reads [anom] itself, as an input of REMBO (see REMBO).

Ocean anomaly from the atmosphere ([ocn_fraction])

The climate modifier after [anom]: the ocean temperature profiles, at every depth, are the reference plus f_to times the mean sea-level air temperature anomaly of the current update over a region (salinity unchanged):

to_ann = to_ann_ref + f_to * mean_region(tsl_ann - tsl_ann_ref)

The anomaly is that of the same update (no lag) and includes [anom]’s dTa; at sea level, it excludes the lapse-rate cooling of the ice sheet.

&ocn_fraction
    active = True
    f_to   = 0.25     ! [1] fraction of the air temperature anomaly
    region = "all"    ! cells of the mean
/

region is "all" or a selection expression of the domain’s regions on the climate grid ([regions], resolved once at init), e.g. "zone:open_ocean", "zone:open_ocean,continental_shelf", "region:Greenland", "~zone:land" (terms layer:values joined by & and |; see the fesm-utils regions docs). Its cell count is printed at init; an empty region stops the run. Refused for a backend without ocean profiles or sea-level air temperature (esm, rembo), and with [anom] f_to /= 0 (both would set the ocean temperature anomaly). The anomaly is written to the climate’s 2D file (ocn_fraction_dto). See Snapshots.

Ocean from the ocean box model ([ocn_obm])

The climate modifier after [ocn_fraction], for yelmox_bipolar: the ocean temperature profiles, at every depth, from the box of the domain’s hemisphere of the ocean box model (northern box tn, southern box ts); salinity unchanged:

absolute:  to_ann = box
anomaly:   to_ann = to_ann_ref + (box - box_init)
&ocn_obm_north
    active = True
    mode   = "absolute"   ! absolute | anomaly
/

The one modifier not applied in the climate step: the driver that owns the box model applies it (climate_mod_ocn_obm) after both domains’ climate steps and before their shelf steps; active is the switch of that exchange (yelmox_bipolar needs [ctrl] active_obm). box_init is the box’s initial value of the par file (tn_init, ts_init), also after a restart. Refused for an ocean at the shelf base (esm), and with [ocn_fraction] or [anom] f_to /= 0 (it replaces the ocean temperature). The box temperature and its anomaly are written to the climate’s 2D file (ocn_obm_theta, ocn_obm_dto).

Regional taper to the reference ([taper])

The climate modifier after [ocn_obm] and before [bias]: the atmosphere towards the reference climate with a static weight field w in [0, 1] on the climate grid,

now = ref + w*(now - ref)

for the monthly air temperature and precipitation, their annual means and the sea-level air temperature (those the backend supplies); w = 1 keeps the climate, w = 0 is the reference (e.g. outside the coverage of a reconstruction). The reference is taken at the current surface: a reference at its own elevation (snapshots: the surface of the reference snapshot) is moved there with the backend’s monthly lapse rate and beta_p, as the climate itself. The ocean is unchanged. Refused for a backend without a reference atmosphere (rembo).

outside sets the climate where w < 1:

  • "ref" (default): towards the reference, as above;

  • "mean": towards the reference plus the mean anomaly of the cells with w = 1, per month,

    tas = tas_ref + w*(tas - tas_ref) + (1 - w)*dT(m)
pr  = w*pr + (1 - w)*pr_ref*f(m)

    with dT(m) the mean temperature difference and f(m) the ratio of the precipitation totals over the cells with w = 1 (both against the reference at the current surface); ta_ann and tsl_ann take the mean of dT(m), pr_ann the ratio of the annual totals. A weight without cells at w = 1 stops the run.

&taper
    active   = True
    var_defs = ""         ! files with the group below, searched in order, then this file
    weight   = "taper_w"  ! var_defs group of w [1]: one 2D field (time = 0 0 0 0 1)
    outside  = "ref"      ! ref | mean: where w < 1, towards the reference (plus the mean anomaly of the cells with w = 1)
/

The weight is a varslice group on the climate grid or remapped online (remap, grid_src); values outside [0, 1] (or missing) stop the run. It is written to the climate’s 2D file (taper_w). input/clim_db.nml holds taper_b18_b20, the weight of the Greenland reconstructions on GreenlandPaleo (1 in their coverage, 0 beyond 100 km; outside = "mean" in yelmox_GreenlandPaleo_lgm.nml and yelmox_GreenlandPaleo_deglac.nml).

Climate bias correction ([bias])

A static spatial correction of the atmosphere, the last climate modifier, applied on the climate grid to the monthly air temperature and precipitation of any backend except rembo (annual fields only), before the move to the surface grid’s elevation:

tas = tas + f_tas*dtas,    pr = pr*(1 + f_pr*(fpr - 1))

The annual means follow (temperature additively, precipitation by the ratio of the annual totals); the reference climate and the ocean are unchanged. The group is optional; without it, or with active = False, nothing changes.

&bias
    active   = True
    var_defs = ""             ! files with the groups below, searched in order, then this file
    dtas     = "bias_dtas"    ! var_defs group: temperature correction [K]; "" = none
    fpr      = "bias_fpr"     ! var_defs group: precipitation factor [1]; "" = none
    f_tas    = 1.0            ! weight of dtas
    f_pr     = 1.0            ! weight of fpr
/

dtas and fpr are varslice groups (as snapshots var_defs): one field for all months (time = 0 0 0 0 1) or 12 monthly fields (time = 0 0 0 0 12), on the climate grid or remapped online (remap, grid_src). The weighted annual fields are written to the climate’s 2D file (bias_dtas_ann, bias_fpr_ann).

Insolation and orbit ([orbit])

The surface models that take the top-of-atmosphere insolation (chion’s ITM and BESSI, smbpal’s ITM) compute it with the insol library (a dependency, libinsol.a), the daily mean in closed form from the orbital elements of Laskar et al. (2004). [orbit] (one per domain, [orbit_north] in yelmox_bipolar) gives the elements; both surface models read it:

&orbit
    method = "time"   ! clock: at the forcing clock time_rel; time: at a fixed time; fixed: ecc, obl, per
    time   = 0.0      ! [yr] model time of the orbit (time; relative to 1950, past < 0)
    ecc    = 0.016702 ! [1]   eccentricity (fixed)
    obl    = 23.4393  ! [deg] obliquity (fixed)
    per    = 102.918  ! [deg] longitude of perihelion (fixed)
    fldr   = "input"  ! folder of the orbital tables
/

clock follows the forcing clock (ts%time_rel: transient insolation with tstep_method = "rel"); time holds the orbit of one time (present day by default, the 1950 orbit); fixed takes the given elements (the defaults are the tables’ at 1950). A fixed orbit is the one exception to the single forcing clock. Each model recomputes its insolation only when the elements change, so once per run unless method = "clock". The par files list method and time; the other keys come from input/yelmox_defaults.nml.

Prescribed ice ([ice_bnd])

Yelmo prescribes the ice thickness in its fixed cells (mask_ice = 1: H_ice = H_ice_ref) and relaxes it towards H_ice_ref in its relaxation cells (tau_relax > 0, with ytopo.topo_rel = -1), set by [yelmo_masks]: a strip at the edge of the region where ice is allowed (border_width = N M: N fixed cells, then M relaxation cells with relax_tau), the expressions mask_ice_fixed and relax, or yelmo.mask_border. [ice_bnd] writes the thickness of an ice history into H_ice_ref in these cells, each step before yelmo_update (step_ice_bnd) and at the cold start; the interior keeps its H_ice_ref (the present-day ice):

file:    H_ice_ref = H_ice(time_rel)        (interpolated; the first or last slice outside the file)
scaled:  H_ice_ref = f * H_ice(slice_time)  (f an index, e.g. before the file starts)
&ice_bnd
    active     = True
    var_defs   = "input/clim_db.nml"  ! files with the group, searched in order, then this file
    method     = "file"               ! file | scaled
    H_ice      = "paleomist_H_ice"    ! var_defs group of the ice thickness [m]
    index      = ""                   ! scaled: the index f ([indices] names)
    slice_time = -21e3                ! [yr] scaled: time of the slice
/

input/clim_db.nml holds paleomist_H_ice (PaleoMIST 1.0, 80-0 ka every 2.5 kyr) and glac1d_H_ice (GLAC-1D, 26-0 ka every 100 yr), the IceHistory products on the ice grid. The forcing clock is time_rel (a constant-time run holds the ice of tstep_const). At the cold start the isostasy reference is taken before (the present-day ice: the prescribed ice of another time is a load). With a time-varying border use [barysealevel] method = "file": under fastiso the prescribed ice would add to the barystatic sea level. H_ice_ref is written to yelmo.nc. Example: yelmox_GreenlandPaleo.nml (GRL-PAL, border_width = 2 4, mask_ice_dynamic = "all").

Configuration

A run is set by these namelist groups. Every key is required (a missing key stops the run), except the keys of an unselected init_method and the [relax] and [opt] groups when their switch in [sim] is off.

The forcing groups have defaults: input/yelmox_defaults.nml declares every key of [indices], [idx_<name>] (template [idx]: the tsgen keys, units, restart_every_df), the snapshot backend ([snap], templates [snap_field], [snap_snapshot], [snap_axis]), the climate modifiers ([anom], [ocn_fraction], [ocn_obm], [taper], [bias]), the orbit of the insolation ([orbit]), [ice_bnd] and [obm_forcing] (yelmox_bipolar), with the domain suffix of a multi-domain driver ([anom_north] to [anom]). A par file lists only what differs; an absent group takes every default (a modifier is then inactive). Each read goes through nml_read(..., defaults_file, defaults_group) (the path in libs/yelmox_nml.f90), and a key not declared there stops the run (nml_validate). scripts/check_par_nml.py checks the yelmo groups against input/yelmo_defaults.nml and these groups against input/yelmox_defaults.nml offline (--dead: keys of the other groups that no source reads). The other yelmox groups below are read without defaults.

Group Sets
[ctrl] the run: its timeline group, calendar years, the restart bundle to start from
timeline group ([ctrl] run_step) the timeline: tstep_method, tstep_const, time_init, time_end, dtt
[domain] the domain: name, physical constants, grid of every component, basin set of the hub (basins) (see Multigrid coupling)
[topo], [regions] the hub topography; the regions, zones, basins and named masks (masks, mask_<name>)
[comps] the components: which are active, with which model, how often
[sim] the conditions of this simulation: cold-start ice state, relaxation, optimization, regional modifications
[relax], [opt] the parameters of the relaxation and the optimization switched on in [sim]
[output] the output files; their intervals are [tm_1D], [tm_2D], [tm_2Dsm], and [tm_rst] for restarts

The models have their own groups ([yelmo] and the Yelmo physics groups, [isos], [barysealevel], [marine_shelf], [surface_chion] + [chion], [smbpal], the climate backend’s, …). In yelmox_bipolar the domain groups carry the hemisphere suffix ([domain_north], [comps_north], [sim_north], [relax_north], [opt_north], [output_north], …); [ctrl] and the timeline are shared.

A minimal skeleton:

&ctrl
    run_step        = "ctrl"            ! timeline group ("ctrl" = this group)
    calendar        = False
    calendar_ref    = 1950.0
    restart         = "None"            ! restart bundle folder, or "None" for a cold start
    tstep_method    = "const"
    tstep_const     = 0.0
    time_init       = 0.0
    time_end        = 15e3
    dtt             = 10.0
/

&comps
    with_ice_sheet  = True
    with_isostasy   = True
    with_climate    = True
    with_surface    = True
    with_shelf      = True
    climate         = "snapshots"
    surface_method  = "chion"
    dt_clim         = 10.0
    dt_isos         = 10.0
/

&sim
    init_method       = "none"
    init_marine_H     = False
    init_kill_shelves = False
    init_time_thrm    = 0.0
    relax             = True            ! uses [relax]
    opt               = True            ! uses [opt]
    use_negis         = False
    scale_glacial_smb = False
    lim_pd_ice        = False
/

[ctrl]

Key Values
run_step group name the group holding the timeline: "ctrl" for [ctrl] itself, or a phase group such as [spinup] or [transient], so one par file can hold several run phases
calendar, calendar_ref bool, [yr CE] timeline in calendar years, against the reference year calendar_ref (ESM runs)
restart folder, "None" the restart bundle to start from; "None" = cold start

The timeline group holds tstep_method ("const": the time advances and the forcing time stays at tstep_const; "rel": the forcing follows the time, e.g. for paleo records; "cal": calendar time), tstep_const, time_init, time_end and the main time step dtt [yr].

A restart bundle is a folder restart-<kyr>-kyr/, written on the [tm_rst] schedule and always at time_end. It holds one restart file per stateful model, the shared sea level and the transient-forcing state (in yelmox_bipolar also the ocean box model, with each domain in a subfolder named after it). Pass restart as an absolute path: the run starts inside its own folder. A restart continues the run bit for bit: the state is restored, not stepped again (the surface restart files carry the annual fields of the last cycle); only the climate is re-derived from it, which equals the run’s own when the restart time lies on its dt_clim cadence (counted from the start of the run, as are dt_isos and the [relax], [opt] time windows).

[comps]

The components: which are active, with which model, how often.

Key Values
with_ice_sheet, with_isostasy, with_climate, with_surface, with_shelf bool components in the coupling sequence: the ice sheet, isostasy, the climate (atmosphere + ocean), the surface (mass balance + temperature) and the shelf base (melt + temperature); with_surface and with_shelf need with_climate
climate snapshots, esm, rembo the climate backend
surface_method chion (default), smbpal, smb_simple, climate chion or smbpal (ITM or PDD), both from the climate’s temperature and precipitation; chion runs the ITM, BESSI or PDD snowpack ([chion] model) in daily steps ([surface_chion], [chion]; ITM’s parameters are the group [chion] nml_itm names: smbpal’s [itm], or for Greenland [itm_chion] (alb_ice = 0.31, calibrated to MAR); BESSI gets the surface shortwave as TOA insolation times trans_sw, the TOA insolation itself (chion’s cloud-proxy longwave), the ice thickness, a constant wind_speed and rel_hum, and the air pressure from the surface elevation; its constants are [chion_const], for Greenland alpha_ice = 0.55, calibrated to MAR; BESSI runs pass surface_chion.time_equil=20, converged within 0.3 Gt/yr of the par files’ 100 yr, which ITM needs; PDD’s parameters are the group [chion] nml_pdd names, [pdd_chion] with pdd_method = "cg05" (Calov and Greve, 2005), smbpal’s PDD; the surface temperature of ITM and PDD is chion’s chion_tsrf_annual, the annual-mean air temperature warmed by firn_fac times the annual refreezing, capped at freezing over ice); smb_simple (needs a sea-level air temperature: snapshots); the climate’s own smb (esm, rembo; required by rembo)
dt_clim [yr] climate update interval; <= 0: updated only at the cold start
dt_isos [yr] isostasy update interval (> 0; 10 in all par files): FastIsostasy integrates the whole interval in one call, the bedrock is held in between

[sim]

The conditions of this simulation: the cold-start ice state (init_*), the relaxation, the optimization and regional modifications. The init_* keys act on a cold start only; relax and opt act on a cold start or a restart, with their times counted from the start of the run (time_init).

Key Values
init_method none, equil, recon, recon_ref cold-start ice state: as initialized; a short equilibration with constant boundaries; the reconstruction recon_path as initial ice where recon_regions selects; the reconstruction as reference ice only
init_equil_time [yr] equil: equilibration time
recon_path, recon_var path, name recon, recon_ref: the reconstruction file ({domain}, {grid_name} = grid_ice) and its ice-thickness variable
recon_time yr recon, recon_ref: model time of the state; the nearest slice of recon_path (its time axis converted from the units: years ago/BP count into the past, kiloyears/ka in thousands)
recon_regions selection expression recon: where its ice is imposed (e.g. "region:North_America")
init_marine_H bool LGM-like marine ice, before init_method
init_kill_shelves bool no ice where the present-day bed is ocean
init_time_thrm [yr] then equilibrate with the topography fixed (0 = off)
relax bool relax the topography towards the reference ([relax])
opt bool optimize the basal friction and the thermal-forcing correction ([opt])
use_negis bool NEGIS basal-friction modification ([negis])
scale_glacial_smb bool reduce negative glacial smb ([glacial_smb])
lim_pd_ice bool extra melt (4 m/yr) outside the present-day ice extent

A spin-up usually sets relax and opt together: the relaxation holds the floating ice and the grounding zone near the observations while the optimization adjusts the basal friction, and then releases them gradually. A run restarted from a spin-up sets both to False to keep the optimized fields fixed.

[relax]

The relaxation of the topography (step_relax). While active, ytopo.topo_rel is topo_rel and its timescale ramps from tau1 to tau2; after time2, the [ytopo] values of topo_rel and topo_rel_tau apply again.

Key Values
topo_rel ytopo.topo_rel mode where the ice relaxes while active (3: all points, 4: the grounding line and grounding zone)
tau1, tau2 [yr] relaxation timescale until time1, and at time2
time1, time2 [yr] end of the tau1 period, and end of the relaxation
m [-] exponent of the ramp from tau1 to tau2

[opt]

The optimization (step_optimize), towards the observed ice thickness, of the basal-friction coefficient cb_ref (opt_cf, between cf_time_init and cf_time_end) and of the thermal-forcing correction tf_corr of the marine shelf (opt_tf, between tf_time_init and tf_time_end):

Key Values
opt_cf none, L21 cb_ref following Lipscomb et al. (2021)
opt_tf none, L21, L21-points one tf_corr per basin (tf_basins), or one per point (tf_sigma, basin_fill)
cf_init value initial cb_ref on a cold start (<= 0: the till friction of the bed, cb_tgt)

The method and its other parameters are described in the Yelmo docs, Basal friction optimization.

[output]

Each module writes its own files, on its own grid, at the [tm_2D] (2D), [tm_2Dsm] (small 2D) and [tm_1D] (time series) intervals:

Key Files
write_yelmo yelmo.nc, yelmo_sm.nc, yelmo_ts.nc (and yelmo_ts_<name>.nc for the named masks of [regions])
write_isos isos.nc, isos_ts.nc
write_shelf mshlf.nc
write_surface the surface model’s file, <surface_method>.nc: chion.nc, smbpal.nc, smb_simple.nc; none with climate (the climate’s file has the smb, smb_ann, tsrf_ann)
write_clim the backend’s file: snap.nc (snapshots), esm.nc + esm_ts.nc, rembo.nc + rembo_ts.nc
write_htopo htopo.nc (the hub)
write_cmip, dt_cmip yelmo_cmip.nc, yelmo_ts_cmip.nc, every dt_cmip years (marine-shelf fields need grid_shelf = grid_ice)
