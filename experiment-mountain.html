Mountain glaciers

Two mountain-glacier domains with the ice sheet at 250 m and no ocean ([comps] with_shelf = False): the Pyrenees over the last glacial cycle, -120 kyr to the present, forced by a spatially homogeneous air-temperature anomaly from an orbital index, and SRG (basin set SanRafael), 200 yr in the present-day climate.

  • Program: yelmox (make yelmox; formerly make yelmox_glaciers) with the snapshot backend ([comps] climate = "snapshots", see Snapshots).
  • Config: yelmox/yelmox_Pyrenees.nml, yelmox/yelmox_SRG.nml.

Running

Run from the yelmox root; there are no scripts (queue, walltime and threads are runme options).

runme -rs -q compute -w 08:00:00 --omp 16 -e yelmox -n yelmox/yelmox_Pyrenees.nml -o output/pyrenees
runme -rs -q compute -w 00:30:00 --omp 16 -e yelmox -n yelmox/yelmox_SRG.nml -o output/srg

Grids

Each component runs on its own grid ([domain], see Multigrid coupling):

Pyrenees SRG
Hub, ice sheet, surface PYR-250M (1520 x 688) SRG-250M (208 x 120)
Climate PYR-2KM (190 x 86), the grid of the FesmData ERA5 product SRG-250M
Isostasy PYR-2KM SRG-16KM (off: [comps] with_isostasy = False)

In the Pyrenees the climate is computed on the 2 km surface and moved to the 250 m surface for the surface model: monthly tas and pr interpolated (bilinear) and corrected for the elevation difference with the lapse rates of [snapshots] lapse and the precipitation scaling f_p. The geothermal heat flux is read on PYR-2KM and remapped onto the Yelmo grid.

Settings that keep the 250 m runs cheap: 6 vertical levels in the ice ([yelmo] nz_aa = 6), no level-set calving front ([ycalv] use_lsf = False), the climate updated every 50 yr in the Pyrenees ([comps] dt_clim = 50; SRG 10 yr; the surface still runs every step), and the climate and isostasy of the Pyrenees on 2 km.

Pyrenees

  • Timeline: [ctrl] -120 kyr to 0, dtt = 10 yr, on relative time (tstep_method = "rel"); [comps] dt_isos = 10 yr.
  • Initial state: cold start from the present-day topography (TOPO-GLO90-GEBCO2026), a 10-yr equilibration with constant boundaries ([sim] init_method = "equil", init_equil_time = 10).
  • Climate: the reference (snapshot_pd, ERA5 1981–2010: tas, pr, zs) plus the uniform term cooling ([term_cooling] on tas and pr, f_ta = -10 K) on the series orbital_t2018 ([series_orbital_t2018] method = "series", input/alpha_orbital_t2018.dat, 1 at present and 0 at 21 ka, normalised with norm_t0 = 0, norm_t1 = -21e3 to 0 at present and 1 at 21 ka): dTa = f_ta * f, the precipitation times exp(f_p dTa) (f_p = 0.05 K-1, the default). The air temperature is the ERA5 reference at present and 10 K below it at 21 ka (+1.4 to -11.1 K over the run). No ocean.
  • Orbit: present day ([orbit] method = "fixed_time", time = 0); the PDD takes no insolation.
  • Sea level: the model’s own ice ([barysealevel] method = "fastiso").
  • Output: 1D every 10 yr, 2D every 1 kyr, small 2D and restart bundles every 10 kyr.

[marine_shelf] stays in the par file with the shelf off: the domain still initializes the marine shelf (no data files are read).

SRG

  • Timeline: [ctrl] 0 to 200 yr, dtt = 5 yr, constant forcing time (tstep_method = "const"). No series or terms ([forcing] series = "", terms = ""), present-day orbit and constant sea level ([barysealevel] method = "const"): the climate is the reference throughout.
  • Initial state: present-day topography (TOPO-GLO90-GEBCO2026, with z_bed_sd), a 10-yr equilibration ([sim] init_method = "equil").
  • Climate: MAR v3.14 1960–1990 on SRG-250M ([snapshot_pd]), a legacy ice_data file not in FesmData; its database groups (srg_mar314_1960_1990_tas, _pr, _zs) are in the par file itself, which is searched before the [forcing] var_defs files. [snapshots] f_p = 0.096 K-1.
  • Relaxation outside the icefield: [yelmo_masks] relax = "~SanRafael:1", relax_tau = 50 yr, with [ytopo] topo_rel = -1: outside basin 1 of the SanRafael set the ice relaxes to the reference thickness; inside it evolves freely. [sim] relax and opt are off ([relax], [opt] unused).
  • Output: 1D every 1 yr, small 2D every 10 yr, 2D every 200 yr, a restart bundle at the end.
